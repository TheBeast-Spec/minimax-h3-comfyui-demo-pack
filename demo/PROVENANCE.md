# Replacement-image provenance

All four reference images were generated for this replacement example using the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on September 18, 2026. They are AI-generated illustrations of fictional people, a fictional vehicle treatment, and a fictional venue, not documentary photographs.

No original film image was passed to image generation. The new Nora and Owen portraits generated for the earlier review draft served only as identity references for the new character sheets. The storyboard used those new sheets as its identity and wardrobe references. The car sheet was generated independently without an input image.

## Generation specifications

- **Nora sheet:** four panels, front-facing face portrait plus front, side, and rear three-quarter full-body views; freckles, black bob, navy sleeveless knee-length dress, silver jewelry, black ankle boots, neutral studio background.
- **Owen sheet:** four panels, face portrait plus front, side, and rear three-quarter full-body views; auburn curls, reddish beard, black suit/shirt/tie, clear right-ear earpiece, neutral studio background.
- **Car sheet:** six studio views of one silver widebody muscle coupe, twin blue center stripes, black wheels, no graffiti, text, or logos.
- **Storyboard:** sixteen equal panels with one consistent nightclub doorway two-shot; Nora left, Owen right, warm amber key and cool blue rim; restrained alternating speaking/listening moments, no crowd or car.

Owen's sheet received a targeted image-generation edit to remove misplaced visible accessory coils from the lower views while retaining the correct frontal portrait. A small pale neck line remains in the side view; the guide and prompt explicitly make the frontal portrait the authority for right-ear placement.

The images were visually reviewed for composition and consistency. Their PNGs were re-encoded from unchanged pixels to remove embedded metadata. File hashes are listed in the pack manifest. File validation is not a validation of generated video quality.
